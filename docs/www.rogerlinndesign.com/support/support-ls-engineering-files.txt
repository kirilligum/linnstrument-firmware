LinnStrument Parts Engineering Files

   I've been impressed with Chris Guichet's "Speedbumpy" alternate playing
   surface for LinnStrument. To help his efforts, I gave him my
   engineering files for the stock playing surface, which gave him a
   better starting point to work from in his design.

   This made me wonder if others with engineering skills might have
   similar ideas for enhancing or personalizing LinnStrument, or even
   selling alternate parts as Chris did. To help any such efforts, I've
   posted below engineering files of the following parts for both
   LinnStrument and LinnStrument 128:

   [60]LinnStrument 200, wood sides engineering files.zip

   [61]LinnStrument 128, wood sides engineering files.zip

   [62]LinnStrument 200, playing surface, engineering files.zip

   [63]LinnStrument 128 playing surface, engineering files.zip

   [64]LinnStrument 200 top panel, engineering files.zip

   [65]LinnStrument 128 top panel, engineering files.zip

   [66]LinnStrument 200 chassis bottom, engineering files.zip (for
   locating screw holes for custom attachments)

   [67]LinnStrument 128 chassis bottom, engineering files.zip (for
   locating screw holes for custom attachments)
