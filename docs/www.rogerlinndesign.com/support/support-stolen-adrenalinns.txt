Stolen AdrenaLinn III Units

   Here is a list of AdrenaLinn III units that have been stolen:

   * Serial number A11726: Fraudulent sale to Jamestown, NY in 2015.

   * Serial number A11727: Fraudulent sale to Williamsville, NY in 2015.

   * Serial number A11728: Fraudulent sale to Rock Springs, WY in 2015.

   * Serial number A11877: Fraudulent sale to North Arlington, NJ in 2017.

   * Serial number A11935: Owner Harley Osterlund of Fort Collins CO
   bought this AdrenaLinn III in 2017. He had a problem in 2018, so I sent
   him a $200 circuit board on the condition that he would subsequently
   return his original circuit board. After repeated requests, he never
   did. I consider this a stolen unit.

   If any AdrenaLinn I, II or III has no serial number label on the back,
   it is also stolen.

   If you encounter one of these stolen units or a unit with no serial
   label, please [39]email me. Also, please report it to the police and
   invite them to contact me for verification.

   Thank you,
   Roger Linn
