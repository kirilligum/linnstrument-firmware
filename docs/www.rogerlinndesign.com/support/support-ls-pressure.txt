How hard should I play LinnStrument?

   LinnStrument should be played with the same range of force used for
   playing chords and melodies on a MIDI piano keyboard.

   If you have a heavier or lighter touch, you can change LinnStrument's
   Velocity and Pressure Sensitivity settings in Global Settings.

   If you are accustomed to using drum pads and striking with very high
   force, this may prematurely wear out LinnStrument's touch sensor sheet,
   resulting in reduced sensitivity in the heavily played areas.

   Thank you,
   Roger
